BTC Live BTC News

Open tooling and public data trackers.

Live BTC News publishes a daily archive of Bitcoin and crypto industry headlines, each one summarized in our own words with a link back to the outlet that reported it. Alongside the archive we run a handful of small reference trackers that compute from public exchange data in the browser, with no account and no API key involved.

This page exists to point at those tools and to hold the code we publish openly. The full set lives in the Live BTC News tools hub.

Calculators

Bitcoin price target calculator

Pick a date and see how far bitcoin would have to move to reach every level from $40,000 to $150,000, the daily and weekly rate that requires, and how much it has actually been moving on an average day. Arithmetic, not a forecast. Open the bitcoin price target calculator

Zcash price target calculator

The same calculation for ZEC, covering every level from $500 to $5,000 in $250 steps, set beside ZEC's recent average daily move. Open the Zcash price target calculator

Trackers

Zcash price and ZEC/BTC ratio

Live ZEC pricing, the ratio against Bitcoin, market cap and a one-year chart, plus a running archive of Zcash news. The ratio view inverts to show how many ZEC one Bitcoin buys, which is a figure almost nobody publishes. Open the Zcash tracker

Bitcoin priced in gold

How many troy ounces of gold one bitcoin buys, updated continuously, with a one-year chart. Pricing in gold removes the dollar from the comparison, so a move that was really about the dollar shows up as a flat line rather than a rally. Open the gold ratio page

Notes on the data

Figures come from public exchange and pricing endpoints, fetched by the reader's own browser rather than routed through a server. Prices may be delayed, the pages are references rather than trading tools, and nothing published is investment advice. The operators may hold positions in assets covered.

Repositories live under github.com/Live-BTC-News. The daily archive is at livebtcnews.com.
